## What Ohayo Prepa is

Ohayo Prepa is a learning platform for high-stakes Malagasy exams. It turns
real past subjects into practice you can actually grade:

- **Past papers, digitised and organised** by exam, field and year.
- **Timed mock tests** in real exam conditions.
- **Automatic correction**, so you know right away where you stand.
- **"Faradoboka" predictions**: the subjects most likely to come up this year.

It starts with the **CNTEMAD** semester exams (every field) and the
**university entrance exams** (ESPA, INSCAE, ENI), with more to follow.

## What it solves

In Madagascar, past papers are scattered, often only on paper, and come
without corrections. Students revise alone, without knowing if their
answers are right. Ohayo Prepa puts every subject in one place and lets
students practise, check and improve.
