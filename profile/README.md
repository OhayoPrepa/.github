<h1 align="center">📚 Ohayo Prepa</h1>

<p align="center">Prepare for Madagascar's big exams with real past papers.</p>

## What it is

Ohayo Prepa turns past exam subjects into practice you can grade: timed mock
tests, automatic correction and predictions of the subjects most likely to
come up. It starts with CNTEMAD semester exams and university entrance
exams (ESPA, INSCAE, ENI).

## What it solves

Past papers in Madagascar are scattered, often on paper, and come without
corrections. Ohayo Prepa puts them in one place and lets students check
their answers right away.
